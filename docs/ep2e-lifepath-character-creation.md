## Overview & Core Rules

Rather than making a character and then improvising a backstory that fits those stats, the Life Path system builds your character chronologically from childhood through adulthood, the Fall, and post-Fall experiences—producing both stats and a cohesive personal story.

This system is loosely based on default character creation (*Making Characters*, p. 36, *EP2*). Familiarize yourself with *Character Stats* (p. 36, *EP2*) before proceeding. At each stage you update your character's stats:
- **Package Steps (Steps 4, 9, 10):** Choose or roll randomly to acquire a package of skills, rep, motivations, traits, or morphs.
- **Event Steps (Steps 5, 7, 8, 12, 13, 14):** Roll `1d100` on an event table for narrative plot points and mechanical modifiers.

> **Dice Notation:** Throughout *Character Options*, `1d10` uses `1–0` (where `0` = 10) and `1d100` uses `00–99` (two ten-sided dice read as tens and units).

### Accumulating Points & Trait Levels

- **Accumulating Points:** Aptitude, skill, and rep points acquired across stages are **cumulative** and summed at [Step 15](#step-15-final-stats-details--starting-gear). For example, `Athletics 10` + `Athletics 30` = `40` points added in Step 15.
- **Point Reductions:** If you have `30 c-rep` and lose `10 c-rep`, you drop to `20 c-rep`. If an event reduces a rep or skill when you currently have `0` points in it, **record the debt/reduction**—subsequent gains in that stat later in the path are reduced by it.
- **Cumulative Traits:** Multi-level traits stack (e.g., `Resources (Level 1)` + `Resources (Level 1)` = `Resources (Level 2)`).
- **Default Trait Levels:** Except for **Psi** and **Resources** (and traits explicitly listing a level), multi-level traits gained in the life path default to **Level 2**. *Optional Rule:* You may randomly determine each multi-level trait's level instead.

### Changing Morphs, Balance & Fairness

- **Changing Morphs:** You acquire a starting morph in [Step 4](#step-4-background-path). Later events (egocasting, resleeving, or death) instruct you to roll on the [Random Morph Tables](#random-morph-tables) or assign a specific morph. **Each new morph replaces your previous morph.** Whatever morph you hold at the end of Step 15 is the one you start gameplay with.
- **Balance and Fairness:** Results are unweighted and neither balanced nor optimized; characters may end up worth more or less Customization Points than standard builds. All rolls are final—no do-overs—unless a result produces a nonsensical mechanical or backstory outcome, in which case talk to your GM about ignoring it and re-rolling.
- **This Is Your Life:** Use each event as a narrative plot point, and look for ways to entangle character histories with fellow PCs who share backgrounds, careers, factions, or events.

### Motivations & Random Motivations

Every character starts with **3 motivations** (*EP2*, p. 47) assigned alongside their background in Step 4. At subsequent steps, consider replacing or updating motivations to reflect how events shaped your outlook.

- Suggested motivations in packages/events are **optional**.
- You may never have **more than 3 motivations**; adopting a new one requires dropping an existing one.
- Motivations are noted as `+` (for/supporting) or `–` (against/opposing). When rolling randomly below, assume `+` unless you prefer to randomize polarity.

### Safety & Optional Flex Rule

- **A Word on Safety:** Event entries include sensitive circumstances (death, violence, coercion, trauma). Treat them as possible explanations rather than mandates—if you dislike an explanation, invent another. If sensitive topics are an issue, ask the GM or another player to screen rolled entries first and re-roll anything you prefer to avoid.
- **Optional Rule — Using Flex:** Your PC starts with 1 ego Flex (and may gain more). If the GM allows, you may spend a point of Flex during character creation to re-roll a stage or event table (each Flex point may only be spent this way once during creation, though it still counts toward your starting Flex total in play). *Hardcore variant:* Re-rolling permanently consumes that point of Flex.

## Step 6: Starting Age

Roll on **Table 6.0 Age** to determine your character's rough age at the beginning of play (for example, in your 20s, 30s, 40s, etc.). To determine an exact age, roll `1d10` for the second digit (e.g., rolling `23` puts your character in their 30s, and a `1d10` roll of `5` makes them 35 at the start of play). Exceptionally young characters skip [Step 7](#step-7-pre-fall-life-event) and go directly to [Step 8: The Fall](#step-8-the-fall).

> **Background Exceptions (`Infolife` / `Lost` / `Uplift`):**
> - **Infolife:** If you acquired the [Infolife](#46-infolife) background in Step 4, your character is in their 20s. Skip directly to [Step 8: The Fall](#step-8-the-fall).
> - **Lost:** If you acquired the [Lost](#48-lost) background, your character is **9 years old**, though you have experienced a subjective **20 + 1d6 years** of combined virtual and real-world life. Skip directly to [Step 9: Career Path](#step-9-career-path).
> - **Uplift:** If you acquired the [Uplift](#410-uplift) background, your character's age **may not exceed 50**. Re-roll higher results.

## Step 15: Final Stats, Details & Starting Gear

Assemble all accumulated modifiers onto your character sheet using these rules:

- **Total Aptitudes and Skills:** List all skills from Background, Career, Interest, Faction, and Event steps. Combine duplicate skills by adding points together (or reassigning excess points to another skill: Know skill points may only go to other Know skills; Active skill points may go to either Active or Know skills). Add linked aptitudes to each skill (`Fray` and `Perceive` add `REF × 2` and `INT × 2`, respectively). **No starting final skill total may exceed `80`** — apply any excess points to another skill (Know points only to Know skills). While you may swap skills at equivalent value, sticking close to acquired skills preserves the Life Path feel.
- **Total Rep:** Combine rep points per network. **No starting rep score may exceed `80`** in any single network; apply excess points to another rep network of your choice.
- **Calculate Derived Stats:**
  - **Initiative:** `(REF + INT) ÷ 5`
  - **Aptitude Checks:** Each Aptitude Check = `Aptitude × 3`
  - **Lucidity (LUC):** `WIL × 2`
  - **Trauma Threshold (TT):** `LUC ÷ 5`
  - **Insanity Rating (IR):** `LUC × 2`
  - **Infection Rating (Asyncs Only):** `Psi trait level × 10`
- **Apply Traits:** Apply all acquired traits. Duplicate traits stack levels (up to maximum value) or may be replaced with a trait of equivalent CP cost/bonus.
- **Add Languages:** Record your native tongue ([Step 3](#step-3-native-tongue)) and any extra languages acquired.
- **Choose Motivations:** Select **3 motivations** from those accumulated across your Life Path (or modified in Step 11).
- **Starting Morph:** Use the morph acquired in [Step 4](#step-4-background-path), or the morph that most recently replaced it via an event roll.
- **Starting Gear:** Take **two gear packs** — one from **Gear Packs: Campaign** (matching your campaign type: Firewall, Gatecrashing, or Guanxi/Criminal) and one from **Gear Packs: Profession** (matching your Step 9 career).

### Worked Character Creation Example

*From* Character Options *pp. 35 and 49. (Note: the source prose omits the numeric roll digit only in Step 7, and Book p. 49 refers to the Step 8 Bruiser morph as `"bouncer"`.)*

1. **Steps 1–3:** Starts with **`1` point of ego Flex** (standard Step 1 total); rolls `1d10 = 6` for the **Survivor** Aptitude Template (`COG 15`, `INT 10`, `REF 15`, `SAV 10`, `SOM 20`, `WIL 20`); rolls `1d100 = 52` for **Korean** native tongue.
2. **Steps 4–8:** Rolls `1d10 = 1` (**Colonist**) → `1d100 = 81` (**Spacer**) → `1d10 = 5` (**Splicer** starting morph); Youth Event `1d100 = 45` grants melting-pot polyglot background (`Hyper Linguistics (Level 2)`); Starting Age `1d100 = 56` (in their **40s**) + `1d10 = 4` = age **`44`** (roughly `32–34` during the Fall, `10` years ago over a `2`-year Fall); Pre-Fall Event rolls `1d100` (prose omits digit; table entry `72`) for a **free skill specialization**; Fall Event `1d100 = 32` stashes their Splicer on Earth and farcasts off-world, then Random Morph `1d100 = 39` (**Biomorph**) resleeves them into a **Bruiser** (`09–10` on Biomorphs; later described as an `"intimidating bouncer"` in the p. 49 summary).
3. **Steps 9–14:** Career `1d10 = 8, 1` (**Investigator**) → `1d100 = 98` (**Journalist**); Interest `1d10 = 8, 3` (**Pilot**); Faction `1d100 = 69` (**Scum**); Post-Fall Event `1d100 = 47` grants **`Identity Crisis`** trait and resleeves into a **Sylph** morph; Crime Event `1d100 = 71` grants Robin Hood (`+10 @-rep`); Story Event `1d100 = 81` sends them on a months-long hedonistic scum swarm bender (printed `"scum warm"` in source).
4. **Step 15 Final Summary (Book p. 49):**
   - **Aptitudes & Checks:** `COG 15` (Check `45`), `INT 10` (Check `30`), `REF 15` (Check `45`), `SAV 10` (Check `30`), `SOM 20` (Check `60`), `WIL 20` (Check `60`).
   - **Derived Stats:** `Initiative 5`, `Lucidity 40`, `Trauma Threshold 8`, `Insanity Rating 80`.
   - **Beginning Combined Skills (before `80` cap):** Duplicate skills summed to `Hardware: Aerospace 70` (`+ COG 15 = 85`), `Know: Flight Crew Ops 100` (`+ COG 15 = 115`), and `Pilot: Space 70` (`+ REF 15 = 85`); base aptitude math includes `Fray 0 + (REF 15 × 2) = 30`, `Perceive 20 + (INT 10 × 2) = 40`, and `Survival (Navigate) 30 (40) + INT 10 = 40 (50)`.
   - **Final Skills (after capping `Hardware: Aerospace` & `Pilot: Space` at `80`, shifting `10` points to `Melee 30`, and capping `Know: Flight Crew Ops` at `80` with `35` points held as an unassigned Know skill):**
     `Fray 30`, `Free Fall 55`, `Hardware: Aerospace 80`, `Infosec 35`, `Interface 45`, `Kinesics 50`, `Know: Current Events 45`, `Know: Flight Crew Ops 80`, `Know: Journalism 75`, `Know: Scum Interests 35`, `Know: Spacers 45`, `Know: Spaceships 35`, `Melee 30`, `Perceive 40`, `Pilot: Air 45`, `Pilot: Ground 35`, `Pilot: Space 80`, `Provoke 30`, `Research 70`, `Survival (Navigate) 40 (50)`, plus `1` unassigned Know skill (`35 + COG 15 = 50`) and `1` unassigned skill specialization (from Step 7).
   - **Rep Scores (`g-rep` summed `10 + 20 = 30`):** `@-rep 30`, `c-rep 40`, `f-rep 20`, `g-rep 30`.
   - **Ego Flex, Traits & Languages:** `1` ego Flex; `Hyper Linguistics (Level 2)` (`+10` to `INT Checks` to grasp the intent of unlearned languages), `Identity Crisis`; Korean + `1` additional language (from Step 4).
   - **Motivations:** `+Self-Reliance`, `+Thrill-Seeking`, `+Uncover Secrets`.
   - **Morph & Gear:** **Sylph** morph; **Investigator** + **Firewall Agent/Criminal** gear packs.
